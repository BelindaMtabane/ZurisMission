# 💧 Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope

**One girl. One bucket. A whole village's hope.**

The stream is dry, the well is broken, and ten-year-old Zuri is done waiting. Armed with a dented bucket and a big dream, she sets out across the wild lands to gather bricks, sand, pipes and parts, dodging wolves, snakes, wild pigs and mud monsters along the way.

Between every level, Zuri becomes a **Water Detective**: she solves water mysteries with her friends, answers quiz questions and learns how to find, protect and share clean water.

A storybook 3D adventure about teamwork, hope and the power of small actions.

*Every drop matters.*

## 🎮 About the Game

Guide Zuri as she rebuilds her village's water system across three runner levels and four story chapters. Collect materials, avoid hazards, solve water mysteries and bring water back to the village.

**Finding Water → Creating Access → Storing and Sharing**

### Game flow

```
Start Screen → Story 1–2 → Level 1 → (win) → Story 3–4 → Level 2 → (win) → Story 5–6 → Level 3 → (win) → Story 7–8 → Start Screen
```

- A new story chapter only opens after a **victory** in the level before it.
- If Zuri loses a level, the level simply restarts.
- **LEVEL 2** and **LEVEL 3** on the Start Screen jump straight to that level's story chapter.

## 📖 Water Detective Stories

Each story chapter has two stories. Every story has a funny opening in three scenes, a three-question quiz and an ending.

| Chapter | Stories | Characters | Leads to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative 1 | 1. The Great Water Mystery · 2. The Hidden Water Clue | Zuri, Villager 1, Villager 2 | Level 1 |
| Narrative 2 | 3. A Drop Goes a Long Way · 4. The Garden Rescue | Zuri, Naledi | Level 2 |
| Narrative 3 | 5. The Forest's Secret · 6. The Pollution Puzzle | Zuri, Kaelo | Level 3 |
| Narrative 4 | 7. The Village Comes Together · 8. Every Drop Counts | Zuri, Villager 1, Villager 2 | Start Screen |

### Quiz rules

- Each story has **3 questions**. Only the **first try** at each question counts towards the score.
- **0–1 correct:** try the quiz again.
- **2 correct:** the story is complete and the journey continues.
- **3 correct:** the second story in the chapter unlocks as a bonus.
- Finishing Story 8 completes the journey and earns the **Water Detective Champion** achievement.

### Skipping and resetting

- After a story has been read once, a **SKIP** button appears on the next visit and takes Zuri straight to the level.
- After a perfect quiz, **SKIP STORY 2 / 4 / 6 / 8** skips the bonus story.
- **RESET** on the Start Screen clears all story and level progress, so the game plays as if for the first time. Sound settings are kept.

## 👥 Characters

- **Zuri (age 10–12):** the brave, funny and slightly dramatic Water Detective
- **Naledi:** Zuri's clever, playful friend who knows all about saving water and gardens
- **Kaelo:** a calm forest guide who teaches Zuri about ecosystems and pollution
- **Villager 1 and Villager 2:** friendly villagers who guide Zuri and become her detective squad

## ⚠️ Hazards

Watch out for these while collecting materials:

- 🐺 **Wolves:** they wait ahead and charge, or chase from behind when time is running out
- 🐍 **Snakes:** slither into a lane after a warning sign appears
- 🐗 **Wild pigs (warthogs):** charge across the path in Levels 2 and 3, so jump over them
- 🟤 **Mud monsters:** hide in mud holes in Level 2 and throw mud
- 🪵 **Rolling logs, cactus walls, rocks, heat waves, acid rain and lightning**, depending on the level

## 🕹️ How to Play

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move left / right between lanes | **A / D** or **← / →** |
| Jump / dodge | **Space** |
| Next line in a story | **NEXT** button |
| Pause | **Pause** button (top left) |

### Tips

- Collect everything on the mission list before heading to the finish.
- Jump over warthogs and rolling logs; switching lanes will not escape a charging warthog.
- Keep an eye on your health, water and materials bars.
- Read the stories carefully. The quiz answers are hidden in the conversations!

## 🎨 Features

- Storybook 3D world that turns from dry and dusty to green and alive
- Eight illustrated Water Detective stories with quizzes, bonus stories and a final champion achievement
- Collect, build and avoid hazards across three levels
- Hand-built village props: desert houses, water tanks, a well, a borehole, rocks, dead trees, logs, cacti, thorn bushes, villagers, wolves, warthogs and mud monsters
- Its own music for each story chapter, plus level music and sound effects

- Real lessons on water conservation, safe drinking water, pollution and how water systems work
