💧 Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope

One girl. One bucket. A whole village's hope.

The stream is dry, the well is broken, and ten-year-old Zuri is done waiting. Armed with a dented bucket and a big dream, she sets out across the wild lands to gather bricks, sand, pipes, and parts, dodging wolves, snakes, and wild pigs along the way.

A storybook 3D adventure about teamwork, hope, and the power of small actions.

Every drop matters.

🎮 About the Game

Guide Zuri as she rebuilds her village's water system in three levels. Collect materials, avoid hazards, and work with the villagers to bring water back.

Finding Water → Creating Access → Storing and Sharing

⚠️ Hazards

Watch out for these cartoon animals while collecting materials:
